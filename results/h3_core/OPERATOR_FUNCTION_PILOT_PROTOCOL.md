The geometry score is deliberately narrow and explicit. It is the Gaussian likelihood of A (early) or A+B (late) under the **fixed far-only carrier atoms ×five operator hypotheses**, with uniform finite prior and sigma.005. No cue-conditioned output is scored to make an exact-posterior claim. These ten-atom weights are exact only for that declared finite prior/likelihood. Their late marginal mean operator gives the shared continuous readout query. Applying those same weights to cue-conditioned C_g outputs is named a pushforward control, not the calibrated H3 posterior. Conditional per-carrier geometry weights are separately softmaxed from log likelihoods to avoid underflow after a carrier's joint mass becomes tiny. All methods receive identical inferred coordinates and witness operators; no GT camera correction is read at inference.

Each head encodes the same three support responses and full24 source carrier into eight channels per support. Inverting the fixed3×3 coordinate design yields24 channels of conditional-function coefficients. This sketch has shape24×7×16×16 (172,032 bytes FP32) per carrier, plus an identical full24 persistent carrier (172,032 bytes). It is a computational response sketch, not a discovered semantic basis or an exact sufficient statistic.

Three mechanically matched heads: mixed19,552 parameters evaluates the conditional coefficients at the query then applies a nonlinear source-conditioned decoder; additive19,627 parameters has separate memory/source and geometry branches with a strictly linear sum, preventing an output nonlinearity from silently adding mixed dependence; joint-set-CNP19,302 parameters concatenates the full coefficient sketch, carrier and query into an unrestricted nonlinear readout. Every head has the same encoder and storage, with copied initial encoder weights and zero-initialized output layers. CNP is the principal strong learned control, not an intentionally path-dependent RNN.
